The Highburrow Nightsoil & Stablemen's Guild
Usually called the Nightsoil Guild, or less charitably the Brown Hand.
* Holds the exclusive charter for removing manure, stable waste, privy waste, and other "noxious materials" within Highburrow and its immediate surroundings.
* Officially exists for sanitation and public health. In practice, it operates as a small, extremely territorial cartel.
* Sets collection rates collectively. Competition on price is considered "uncollegial."
* Controls access to the best dumping, composting, and resale arrangements—including the Brotherhood of the Brown Buffalo contract.
* Members absolutely will not touch another member's route without permission.
* Owns several communal carts, draft ponies, sheds, shovels, barrels, and an alarming amount of lime.
* Has successfully convinced Highburrow's government that unlicensed dung removal constitutes a serious public-health menace.
* Apprenticeship is tightly controlled. Willet Underbrewer is registered as Bogrin's apprentice and therefore cannot legally operate his own route.
* Bogrin is a respected journeyman/member and takes the dignity of the profession extremely seriously.
* Guild meetings are held monthly at The Fat Pheasant, nominally because it has a private room.
* Their motto is “From Burrow to Barrow.”
* Their heraldic device is a golden shovel upon a brown field.
* Everyone laughs at the Nightsoil Guild right up until their stable hasn't been cleared for two weeks.
